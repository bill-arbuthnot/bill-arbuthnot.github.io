Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

"n8n Integration" is a personal app used only by its owner, Bill Arbuthnot, to let his self-hosted n8n automation server access his own Google account.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheets and sends email from the owner's own Gmail account.

How the data is used and stored

Data is processed only on the owner's own computer to run his personal workflows. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
